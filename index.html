Logos Apps

Public repositories for vpavlin's Logos apps — QAKU (Q&A), KYM (budget), Perun.

📟 Basecamp (desktop modules)

In Logos Basecamp → Settings → Package Repositories, add:

https://vpavlin.github.io/logos-apps/basecamp/logos-repo.json

📱 Android (F-Droid)

In the F-Droid app → Settings → Repositories → +, add:

https://vpavlin.github.io/logos-apps/fdroid/repo

Scan in the F-Droid app to add the repo (verified by fingerprint).
